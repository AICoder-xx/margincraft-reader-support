MarginCraft · Offline Monograph Studio (v1.0.0)

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026 · Package ID: com.margincraft.codexreader

1. Publisher & Contact

MarginCraft is published under MarginCraft Press as a local-first monograph reader and marginalia notebook for iOS, iPadOS, and Android. For privacy questions or support, email korysausedan@gmail.com.

2. On-Device Sandbox Storage

Books you import, chapter reading positions, paragraph bookmarks, margin glosses, key-term flashcards, focus session logs, and typesetting preferences are stored exclusively inside the private application sandbox on your device (via React Native AsyncStorage local container files). MarginCraft requires no user account, sign-in, or cloud synchronization.

3. Zero Network Permissions & Zero Tracking

MarginCraft 1.0.0 operates completely offline, declares zero internet or network permissions in its Android release manifest, and connects to zero external servers. The application embeds no analytics SDKs, no crash-telemetry collectors, no advertising networks, and no cross-app tracking identifiers.

4. User-Initiated Shares & Portable Archives

When you choose Share Markdown, your operating system opens its native share sheet; only the recipient or destination app you explicitly choose receives that digest. When you choose Export CRC-32 Bundle, a UTF-8 JSON archive of your shelf and notes is generated locally so you decide whether and where to store a copy.

5. Support Correspondence

If you contact korysausedan@gmail.com using your email client, we receive your reply address and the diagnostic details you choose to include solely to assist with your inquiry. Please omit private manuscript passages unless necessary to reproduce an issue.

6. Retention & Deletion

Local reading records remain on your device until you remove individual items in Library or Margins, reset to the sample shelf in Colophon, clear storage, or uninstall MarginCraft. Exported digests and archives saved outside the app are managed wherever you stored them.

To request deletion of your support correspondence, write to the support mailbox listed above from the address you used to contact us.
