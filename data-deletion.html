Delete Your Reading Data

MarginCraft 1.0.0 stores no cloud accounts; all data resides locally on your device.

1. Delete Individual Volumes, Notes, or Cards

In Library, tap Edit on a volume, select Remove, and tap Confirm Delete to erase that monograph along with its linked margin glosses and lexicon terms (at least one volume remains on the shelf). Individual glosses, lexicon cards, and bookmarks can be removed anytime in Margins.

2. Reset to the Starter Sample Shelf

In Colophon, tap Restore Starter Monograph Shelf… and confirm. This replaces all imported books, margin glosses, bookmarks, lexicon terms, study session logs, and typesetting preferences with the four sample monographs. Export an archive in Margins first if you want a backup.

3. Remove All Local Application Storage

On iOS or iPadOS, open Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → MarginCraft and choose Delete App (Offload App preserves documents and data). On Android, open Settings → Apps → MarginCraft → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

4. Copies Saved Outside MarginCraft

Markdown digests and CRC-32 JSON archives exported or shared outside MarginCraft are controlled by the destination where you saved them and must be deleted separately. Support inquiries: korysausedan@gmail.com.
