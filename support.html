MarginCraft Support

Version 1.0.0 · iOS, iPadOS & Android

1. Importing Plaintext & Markdown Manuscripts

In Library, tap + Import Text to paste any UTF-8 manuscript. Lines starting with #, ##, or ### automatically split long texts into navigable chapters, or tap Load Sample to test with the built-in horology excerpt.

2. Dual-Column Broadsheet, Flashcards & Digest

Open any monograph in Reader and tap Broadsheet to inspect numbered paragraphs beside Vermilion, Cobalt, and Sage margin notes, review chapter word lengths, practice volume-scoped flashcards, or share a Markdown study digest.

3. Verifying & Restoring Portable Archives

In Margins → Archive, tap Export CRC-32 Bundle to generate a UTF-8 IEEE CRC-32 JSON backup. Paste a bundle into the editor and tap Verify Checksum to inspect its integrity non-destructively before confirming Restore Shelf.

4. Contact Support

Email korysausedan@gmail.com with your OS version, app version (1.0.0), and steps to reproduce the issue using one of the four built-in sample monographs.
